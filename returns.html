Delivery & returns.

01. Delivery at launch

Before accepting payment, each eligible product will need clear delivery availability, shipping charges, dispatch estimates and fulfilment responsibility. Foundly is considering a seller-managed fulfilment model initially; the operational arrangement remains subject to testing.

02. Returns and exchanges

The final policy will explain how customers initiate returns, where goods should be sent, how condition and eligibility are assessed, and who pays shipping where permitted by law. Seller preferences cannot remove mandatory consumer rights.

03. Refund processing

Once payments become available, Foundly will need a defined refund workflow that coordinates customers, sellers and the approved payment provider. Refund timing, disputes and any deductions will be disclosed clearly before checkout.

04. Your statutory protections

South African consumer law provides rights relating to defective, unsafe and unsuitable goods, as well as rights that may apply to electronic transactions. The precise remedy and applicable period depend on the circumstances and legal requirements. Our final policy must comply with the Consumer Protection Act and Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

05. What must happen next

Foundly will finalise fulfilment responsibilities, delivery service levels, seller obligations, customer support, dispute handling and refund integrations before enabling real orders. This page will be replaced by a complete, approved customer policy at that stage.
