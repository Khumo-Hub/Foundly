Can I buy products on Foundly now?+

Not yet. You're exploring an interactive frontend preview. The products, prices and example stores are illustrative, and checkout is deliberately unavailable until secure payments, fulfilment and consumer policies are ready.

What can I find on Foundly?+

Our initial concept focuses on three categories: fashion and accessories, homeware and décor, and beauty accessories. The range can evolve as genuine independent sellers join.

Will Foundly only serve Gauteng?+

Gauteng is our proposed first market. Our ambition is to expand across South Africa once marketplace operations are tested and reliable.

How will I know whether a seller is trustworthy?+

Seller onboarding, identity checks, clear product information, order support and transparent policies are planned parts of the real marketplace. Specific verification criteria will be published before live trading.

What about deliveries, refunds and returns?+

No deliveries or returns take place during this preview. Before launch, we will explain who fulfils each order, the estimated delivery time, costs, return steps and statutory consumer rights. See our Returns & deliveries page for the current draft approach.

02 / FOR SELLERS

Made for your
next chapter.

Who will be able to sell on Foundly?+

Our focus is independent small businesses in Gauteng, starting with sellers in the three launch categories. Eligibility and onboarding requirements will be confirmed before accepting live applications.

How much will it cost to sell?+

Foundly is intended to earn a commission on completed marketplace sales. The exact rate, payment processing fees, refund deductions and settlement timeline are not yet final and will be made clear before any seller signs up to trade.

Can I create my shop today?+

You can explore the seller landing page and test its sample interest form. It does not send or store your details. A secure application process will be introduced later.

Will Foundly handle inventory and delivery?+

Our working model is that independent sellers maintain their own products and stock, with fulfilment arrangements defined before transactions begin. We will test the operational model with an initial seller cohort.

What products are not permitted?+

We plan to exclude unlawful, unsafe, counterfeit, prohibited or otherwise restricted products. A detailed seller and product policy will be approved before launch.
