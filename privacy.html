Privacy matters.

Prototype privacy notice · Draft for review · Prepared 4 November 2026

Current preview only. There are no live user accounts, order records or seller applications. This notice describes the frontend demonstration and is not a substitute for the comprehensive POPIA notice required before a real marketplace launch.

01. Temporary browser data

The preview uses your browser’s sessionStorage to remember items added to the demonstration bag and products marked as favourites. This is device/browser-tab storage for interface testing, not a server-side shopping account. The site can function without this storage; your browser settings can also clear it.

02. Demonstration seller form

The seller interest form validates what you type in the browser and shows a sample confirmation. The prototype code does not submit, email or save the information you enter. Do not enter sensitive personal or commercial information into a demonstration form.

03. Website infrastructure and fonts

Hosting providers may process routine technical information needed to deliver the website, such as network requests and basic security logs. This design currently requests fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may contact that provider when loading pages. Foundly has not added a bespoke tracking or analytics tool to this frontend prototype.

04. What changes at a real launch?

A live marketplace would need to process information for user accounts, seller verification, product listings, orders, delivery, fraud controls, payments and support. Before those services are activated, Foundly must define its responsible party, lawful processing purposes, service providers, retention periods, security measures and a real contact channel in a full privacy notice.

05. Your privacy rights

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) provides rights relating to personal information, including access and correction in applicable circumstances. See the Information Regulator’s POPIA resources for authoritative information. Foundly’s final privacy contact details and request process will be published before collecting live customer or seller data.
